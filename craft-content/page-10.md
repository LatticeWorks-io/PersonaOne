            ## What to do
            
            Final warmup days.
            
            **Day 11-14:**
            
            - 4-5 SFW tweets per day
            - 10-15 replies per day, including replies to bigger accounts (10K+ followers)
            - 1-2 selfies per day, more curated
            - Begin liking adult-creator accounts (NOT NSFW posts yet — their SFW posts only)
            
            ## Why 'begin liking adult-creator accounts'
            
            Twitter's recommendation engine looks at WHO you engage with. Liking adult creators' SFW posts signals 'this account is interested in this niche' — improves visibility to that audience LATER once you post NSFW.
            
            ## Anti-pattern
            
            Don't follow 100 NSFW accounts in one sitting. 5-10 per day. Looking organic matters.
            
            ## Verification
            
            Days 11-14: 16-20 more tweets, 40-60 replies, organic engagement growth.

          <title>95 — Day 10: Audit account health</title>
          <content>
            ## What to do
            
            Check for shadowban / restriction signals:
            
            ### Visibility test
            
            - Search Twitter for one of your tweets exactly — does it appear?
            - Yes: good. No after 24 hrs: possible shadowban.
            
            ### Impressions test
            
            - Tweets should get 50-300+ impressions each (Twitter analytics)
            - Flat-lining at 0-20: possible shadowban
            
            ### DM test
            
            - Send yourself a test DM from a separate real Twitter account
            - Does it land in inbox or message requests?
            - Neither: possible DM restriction
            
            ### Algorithmic surface test
            
            - Search a niche keyword you'd want to be found for
            - Scroll 'People' tab — do you appear?
            - Never after 30 min scroll: not in algo graph yet
            
            ## If shadowbanned
            
            Don't panic. Common for new accounts:
            
            - Stop posting 24-48 hours
            - Reduce reply frequency
            - Wait 3-7 days, recheck
            
            ## Verification
            
            Audit results documented in 1Password under `LW-Twitter warmup audit Day 10`.

            ## What to do
            
            **Day 7-10:**
            
            - 3-4 SFW tweets per day
            - 5-8 replies per day
            - 1 additional SFW selfie per day (variety of poses, settings — all SFW)
            - Start using niche hashtags on 30-50% of posts
            
            ## Hashtag strategy
            
            Goal: discoverability without spamminess.
            
            - 2-3 niche tags per tweet, NOT 10+
            - Mix specific tags with broader (e.g., `#altgirl` + `#nyc`)
            - Don't use `#nsfw` or explicit-signal tags yet — that's post-ACC
            
            ## Verification
            
            Days 7-10: 12-16 more tweets. Engagement starting to compound.

            ## What to do
            
            **Day 3-5:**
            
            - 2-3 SFW tweets per day
            - Reply to 3-5 tweets per day (real, substantive replies — 'your cat is gorgeous omg' not '🔥🔥🔥')
            - Follow 5-10 more niche accounts per day
            - Quote-tweet 1 tweet per day with a real opinion
            
            ## SFW content ideas
            
            - Outfit posts (clothed, vibe descriptors)
            - Aesthetic photos (room, coffee shop, sunset)
            - Hot-take tweets (e.g., for alt-niche: `tattoos > piercings dont @ me`)
            - Engagement bait (e.g., `last person you dmd, post their pfp`)
            
            ## Anti-pattern
            
            Don't post a selfie yet. Hold for Day 6+.
            
            ## Verification
            
            By end of Day 5: ~10-15 tweets, ~20-30 replies, ~50-100 likes, following ~30-50 accounts.

            ## What to do
            
            You're inhabiting the persona. Twitter wants to see a HUMAN, not a bot.
            
            **Day 1:**
            
            - Post 1 SFW tweet (e.g., `coffee or no work today ☕`)
            - Like 5-10 tweets from accounts you followed in Page 09 Card 89
            - DO NOT reply to anyone yet
            
            **Day 2:**
            
            - Post 1-2 SFW tweets
            - Like 10-15 tweets
            - Reply to ONE tweet (low-effort: 'lol' or 'saame')
            
            ## Why this pacing
            
            New accounts that immediately spam get flagged. Real humans post sporadically and engage casually as they 'find their voice'.
            
            ## Verification
            
            Day 1-2: 1-2 tweets, casual likes, 1 reply. No NSFW. No automation.
